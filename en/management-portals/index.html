Does the portal show Logo or Mikro data live?

Yes. The portal runs on your ERP data. We decide together which tables sync and how often.

Who can use the portal?

You define the permissions. Each user sees only the data you open to them.

Can you move our existing reports?

Yes. We review the reports you use and offer the same or a better breakdown in the portal.

Does it work with Excel and Power BI?

Yes. You can export reports to Excel and feed Power BI dashboards with the same data.
