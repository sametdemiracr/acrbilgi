KVKK notice

Information under Personal Data Protection Law no. 6698.

Book a meeting

Under Personal Data Protection Law no. 6698 ("KVKK"), ACR Bilgi Sistemleri cares about the security of your personal data.

This text explains why your personal data is processed, who it may be shared with and what rights you have.

Scope

This notice applies to:

  • Website visitors
  • Customers
  • Prospective customers
  • Business partners
  • Suppliers

Personal data we process

In the course of our activities the following personal data may be processed.

  • Identity data: full name
  • Contact data: phone, email, company
  • Transaction security data: IP address, browser information, log records
  • Request data: contact form contents, support requests, quote requests

Purposes of processing

Your personal data is processed for these purposes:

  • Preparing quotes
  • Providing support services
  • Managing customer relations
  • Managing contract processes
  • Accounting and finance operations
  • Meeting legal obligations
  • Ensuring information security
  • Improving service quality

Transfer of data

In line with KVKK, personal data may be transferred to:

  • Public bodies authorised by law
  • Official authorities where legally required
  • Technical infrastructure providers we use

Storage and security

Personal data:

  • Is protected against unauthorised access
  • Is kept with up-to-date security systems
  • Is stored for the legal period
  • Is securely deleted or anonymised afterwards

Your rights under KVKK

Under Article 11 of KVKK you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed
  • Access the data processed
  • Ask for incomplete or wrong data to be corrected
  • Request deletion or destruction of data
  • Object to processing
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage

How to apply

You can send your KVKK requests to info@acrbilgi.com. Applications will be assessed and concluded within the periods set by KVKK.
