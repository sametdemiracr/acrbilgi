Privacy policy

How we protect the information shared through our website.

Book a meeting

At ACR Bilgi Sistemleri we care about our visitors' privacy. This Privacy Policy explains why personal data shared through www.acrbilgi.com is processed, how it is protected and when it may be shared with third parties.

The information you share when you visit our website or use our contact forms is used only to provide services, assess your requests and get in touch with you.

Scope

This policy covers:

  • Website visitors
  • Users who request a quote
  • Customers who open a support request
  • Visitors who use the contact form

Data we process

The following information may be processed through our website:

  • Full name
  • Company name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Request or message content
  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Cookie data

Why we use the data

The information we collect is used to:

  • Assess quote requests
  • Run technical support processes
  • Manage customer relations
  • Develop our services
  • Analyse website performance
  • Meet legal obligations

Storage and security

ACR Bilgi Sistemleri applies up-to-date technical and administrative security measures to protect personal data. Data is:

  • Protected against unauthorised access
  • Stored on secure servers
  • Backed up regularly
  • Kept encrypted where needed

Cookies

Cookies may be used on our website to improve the user experience. They are used to measure site performance, analyse traffic and remember user preferences. You can change cookie use at any time in your browser settings.

Third-party services

The following third-party services may be used on our website. They may process data under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Analytics
  • Google Ads
  • Microsoft Clarity
  • reCAPTCHA

Contact

For questions about our privacy policy, write to info@acrbilgi.com. When legal retention periods end, personal data is securely deleted or anonymised.
