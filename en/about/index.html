What we do

ACR Bilgi Sistemleri is an authorised Mikro Yazılım partner. We handle sales, setup, adaptation and support of the Mikro product family. We also cover servers, licences, networks and custom software, because in practice they cannot be separated.

How we work

  1. We write the scope downWe do not start on a verbal agreement. What will and will not be done, the timeline and the price are in writing.
  2. We do not sell what you do not needWe do not recommend a module you do not need or a plan bigger than necessary. Sometimes the right answer is "you do not need that now".
  3. We do not create dependenceThe source code and documentation of the software we build are yours. If we part ways, your system keeps running.
